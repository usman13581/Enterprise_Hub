# Enterprise Hub — Website subscription plans brief

Share this with the website agent to implement the pricing / plans section.

## Currency
All public prices are in **USD**.

## Plans to display

### 1. Basic
- **Monthly:** `$49 / month`
- **Annual:** `$499 / year` (save vs monthly)
- **Users:** **1 user only**
- **CTA:** Start free trial / Sign up (or existing website CTA)
- **Badge ideas:** “Solo”, “Starter”

### 2. Standard
- **Monthly:** `$99 / month`
- **Annual:** `$949 / year`
- **Users:** **Up to 10 users**
- **CTA:** Start free trial / Sign up
- **Badge ideas:** “Most popular”, “Teams”

### 3. Custom
- **Price:** Do **not** show a dollar amount
- **Label:** **Contact sales team**
- **Users:** Flexible / negotiated (enterprise)
- **CTA:** Contact sales (mailto / form / Calendly — use existing sales channel)
- **Badge ideas:** “Enterprise”, “Tailored”

## Billing toggle (recommended UX)
- Monthly | Annual switch on the pricing page
- Annual prices:
  - Basic → `$499/year`
  - Standard → `$949/year`
- Custom stays “Contact sales” on both toggles

## Suggested feature bullets (align with product; refine with marketing)

**Basic (1 user)**
- Full company workspace for a single owner/operator
- Quotations, jobs, invoices, advances
- Customers, products, suppliers
- Purchase orders & purchase invoices
- Reports & PDF documents
- Mobile app access

**Standard (up to 10 users)**
- Everything in Basic
- Team seats (up to 10 users)
- Role-based access (admin / member)
- Shared company data across the team
- Suitable for growing marble / stone businesses

**Custom**
- Everything in Standard
- Custom seat counts and commercial terms
- Onboarding / training support as agreed
- Priority support options
- Tailored packaging for multi-branch or enterprise needs

## Technical notes for website agent
- Product codes used in the platform (for deep links / signup if needed):
  - `basic` (monthly), `basic-yearly` (annual)
  - `standard` (monthly), `standard-yearly` (annual)
  - `custom` (contact sales — no self-serve checkout)
- Demo / trial may still exist separately as a free trial flow; do not list demo plans as paid tiers on the public pricing page.
- Do not show AED pricing on the marketing site.

## Layout guidance
- Three equal cards: Basic | Standard | Custom
- Highlight Standard as the recommended / most popular plan
- Annual toggle above the cards
- Keep first viewport clean: brand + headline + short subhead + CTA; put pricing cards in the next section unless the page *is* the pricing page
